# AGENCE DAPHNÉ
**DIGITAL STUDIO · FRANCE**

### Design. Technology. Intelligent Growth.

We build digital experiences that connect thoughtful design, modern development and intelligent business solutions.

Based in Haguenau, France, **Agence Daphné** brings together web development, digital strategy, AI-powered solutions and creative production.

Our focus is simple: build digital experiences that look considered, work reliably and serve a clear business purpose.

---

## What We Do

### 01 — Web Design & Development
Custom websites, responsive interfaces, landing pages and website redesigns, with development capabilities in **Next.js and React**.

### 02 — E-commerce & Integrations
Online shopping experiences, product presentation, customer journeys and integrations connecting websites with business tools.

### 03 — AI Chatbots & Automation
AI-powered customer interactions, conversational assistants and automated workflows designed to simplify processes and support customer engagement.

### 04 — SEO & GEO
Technical SEO, local search visibility, website performance and content structures that help businesses improve their presence across traditional and AI-powered search.

### 05 — Daphné Studio
Creative direction, visual identities, product imagery, digital campaigns, motion design and AI-assisted visual production.
